Yang (Aurelia) Li

I am a first-year M.A. student in economics at Simon Fraser University, working in labor and gender economics. My research asks how institutions and technology shape the choices of ordinary people: a grandparent whose pension determines whether her son can migrate, a job applicant screened out by a model trained on decades of biased hiring.
